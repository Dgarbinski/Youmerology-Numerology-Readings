Numerology FAQ

Questions people ask when they are searching for meaning.

Can numerology be used for spiritual guidance?

Many people use numerology as a reflective spiritual practice to explore personal themes, relationships and timing. It can provide prompts for self-inquiry, but it is not a scientifically validated method for predicting events or guaranteeing outcomes.

What is a Life Path number?

A Life Path number is derived from your birth date. In numerology traditions, it is interpreted as a broad description of recurring themes, strengths and lessons in your life.

What are Personal Year, Month and Day numbers?

They are date-based cycles used in numerology to reflect on changing themes over different time scales. Youmerology lets you view them together and move to different dates.

Can Youmerology check relationship compatibility?

Yes. Youmerology compares numerology information for two people and presents strengths, possible friction points and themes for growth.

Can I look at future dates?

Yes. You can explore past, present and future dates using Personal Year, Month and Day cycles, Periods, Pinnacles and the visual timeline.

Is numerology scientifically proven?

No. Numerology is a traditional interpretive system rather than a scientifically validated method of prediction. Youmerology is designed for self-reflection, personal insight and entertainment.
