Numerology FAQ

Common questions.

What is a Life Path number?

A Life Path number is a numerology value derived from your birth date. Numerology traditions use it to describe broad themes, tendencies and lessons associated with a person's life journey.

What are Personal Year, Month and Day numbers?

These are date-based numerology cycles used to interpret changing themes over different time scales. Youmerology lets you view them together and move to different dates.

Can Youmerology check relationship compatibility?

Yes. Youmerology includes compatibility insights that combine numerology information for two people and highlight strengths, friction points and potential areas for growth.

Can I look at future dates?

Yes. The forecast tools are designed to explore past, present and future dates, including Personal Year, Month and Day cycles, Periods, Pinnacles and the visual timeline.

Is numerology scientifically proven?

Numerology is a traditional interpretive system, not a scientifically validated method for predicting events. Youmerology is designed for self-reflection, personal insight and entertainment rather than guaranteed outcomes.
