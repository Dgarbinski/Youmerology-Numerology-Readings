Life Path numerology guide

What does your Life Path number mean?

Your Life Path number is one of the most widely used birth-date calculations in numerology. It is traditionally read as a broad theme for your strengths, lessons and direction.

What is a Life Path number?

A Life Path number is calculated from the digits in your date of birth and reduced to a single digit, with 11, 22 and 33 often preserved as master numbers. Numerology traditions interpret the result as a symbolic description of recurring tendencies and lessons.

Life Path meanings at a glance

Life Path 1 — The Initiator

Independence, initiative, originality and learning to trust your own direction.

Life Path 2 — The Diplomat

Cooperation, sensitivity, patience and learning how to work with other people without losing yourself.

Life Path 3 — The Communicator

Creativity, expression, optimism and bringing ideas, feelings or stories into the open.

Life Path 4 — The Builder

Structure, discipline, dependability and creating stable foundations through steady effort.

Life Path 5 — The Explorer

Freedom, adaptability, change and learning through direct experience.

Life Path 6 — The Nurturer

Responsibility, care, harmony and balancing service to others with your own needs.

Life Path 7 — The Seeker

Analysis, introspection, intuition and a drive to understand what lies beneath the surface.

Life Path 8 — The Executive

Ambition, organization, material responsibility and learning how to use influence constructively.

Life Path 9 — The Humanitarian

Compassion, completion, perspective and contributing to something larger than yourself.

What are master numbers 11, 22 and 33?

Some numerology systems keep 11, 22 and 33 unreduced because they are treated as master numbers. They are commonly associated with heightened versions of themes found in 2, 4 and 6 respectively.

  • 11: intuition, inspiration and sensitivity.
  • 22: turning large ideas into practical form.
  • 33: service, compassion and teaching.
